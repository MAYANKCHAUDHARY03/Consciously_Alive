A premium academic knowledge and intelligence platform for structured weekly current affairs, general awareness, defence updates, editorials, vocabulary, OIR practice, and revision.

Overview

Weekly General Awareness is a full-stack academic intelligence platform designed to turn scattered weekly information into a structured, searchable, and revision-friendly knowledge system.

The platform follows a briefing-oriented workflow inspired by the discipline of professional intelligence and defence briefings:

Collect → Organize → Review → Synthesize → Revise

Instead of relying on scattered notes, WhatsApp messages, PDFs, and saved links, the application maintains a centralized weekly knowledge archive powered by Notion.
